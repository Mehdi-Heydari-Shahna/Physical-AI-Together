# The protocol of the published algorithms (Section 6.2.4)

Written on 27 October 2026, before any run of `train_sac.py`, `train_tdmpc2.py` or `train_diffusion.py`. The only other runs, before and after it, are the validation of the environment (`test_cart_env.py`) and the test of the scripts' control flow with stand-ins for the libraries (`test_harness.py`); neither trains a published algorithm. The scripts and `report.py` implement this protocol as written here. A change made after a run will be listed under *Deviations* at the end, with its reason, and the results under the protocol as it stood before the change will be reported as well. The practice is that of `code/ch16/HYPOTHESES.md`.

## What is run

Three published implementations, each through the adapter that Section 6.2.1 describes for its family, on the cart of Chapter 6:

| Method | Implementation | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| SAC | `stable_baselines3.SAC` of Stable-Baselines3 2.9.2 (Raffin et al., JMLR 2021) | the library's default hyperparameters, policy `MlpPolicy`; only the seed and the device are set |
| TD-MPC2 | the official code, github.com/nicklashansen/tdmpc2 at commit `332f19e4aba4bffe2dc736f45ea7f7bdc585903a` | its `config.yaml` with the default hyperparameters and `model_size=5`, read by its own `parse_cfg`; `episodic=true`, because the task ends at arrival; `rho=0.7` and `discount_max=0.99`, which the official code sets for its episodic Gymnasium tasks (`envs/mujoco.py`); `obs_shape`, `action_dim`, `episode_length=200` and `seed_steps=1000` derived as its `make_env` derives them; the loop of its `OnlineTrainer`, ended by a number of episodes |
| Diffusion policy | the authors' code, github.com/real-stanford/diffusion_policy at commit `5ba07ac6661db573af695b419a7947ecb704690f`: `DiffusionUnetLowdimPolicy` with `ConditionalUnet1D` | the settings of `train_diffusion_unet_lowdim_workspace.yaml` (U-Net 256-512-1024 with kernel 5 and FiLM scale, 100 DDPM steps with the squared-cosine schedule and epsilon prediction, 100 inference steps, the observation as global condition, EMA with power 0.75, AdamW with learning rate 1e-4 and the cosine schedule with 500 warm-up steps, batch 256, 5000 epochs), except T_o = 1, T_p = 8 and T_a = 4, as for the chunk imitator |

The stand-ins and the baselines of Section 6.2.2 are not run again: their evaluation runs are those of `../results/evaluation.csv`, which `test_cart_env.py` reproduces, run by run, through the environment used here (`results/validation.txt`).

## The task, the worlds and the environment

Everything is that of Section 6.2.2 and `../cart_learning.py`, imported unchanged: the world family (the start of the ramp in [17, 21] m, a triangle-wave disturbance below 0.05 m/s²), the forecasting predictor, whose labels are valid in every world of the family, the tube package of Sections 5.1 and 5.2, the governor, the reserve floor (0.5 m before the wall at 25 m, so the cart must stay at or before 24.5 m), the period of 0.1 s and the task: reach 24 m within 20 s. Every run then continues until the cart is at rest, at most until 25 s; nothing after arrival enters a learner's records or the mean intervention. `cart_env.CartGovernedEnv` runs one world per episode with the Gymnasium API; in every period the governor answers the proposal.

- **Observation** (the same for the three methods): the observation of Table 6.1 (position, velocity, label summary with a flag for none within reach, level of the plan in execution, committed ambition, alarm) and two features computed from it, the speed v_env of Section 6.1.2 and whether the cart is on the low-braking region, as float32, divided by fixed scales.
- **SAC and TD-MPC2** propose one reference acceleration per period, the action in [-1, 1] standing for [-1.875, 1.875] m/s², resolved to 0.001 m/s²; the generator blends it with the head of the fallback on the ambition grid. TD-MPC2 plans over three steps and executes the first action of its plan, as published, so its proposal is a single reference too. Its interventions therefore compare with those of SAC, the off-policy learner, the rule and the braking curve, and not with those of the planner with a learned model, which proposes sequences of eight.
- **Reward** (SAC and TD-MPC2): that of the off-policy learner, the executed reference acceleration of the next period divided by 1.875 m/s², minus 0.5 times the intervention. For the learner, an episode ends at arrival (a termination) or at 20 s (a truncation); the environment then finishes the run with the current policy acting deterministically (SAC's deterministic action, TD-MPC2's planner in evaluation mode), outside the replay buffer.
- **The diffusion policy** proposes a chunk of eight references in m/s²; the generator offers its prefixes as for the chunk imitator. `protocol.ChunkPolicy` serves the chunk as the chunk imitator does: the remainder of the chunk, padded with its last reference, in the following periods, and a new chunk after four periods or after an intervention. Its records pair the observation with the expert's chunk at that state, the demonstrated action and not the executed one (Section 6.3.3).

## Budgets

Chosen now, before any run:

- **SAC and TD-MPC2, budget M: 40 training episodes**, in the worlds 100 s, ..., 100 s + 39 for seed s, the stand-ins' budget in the stand-ins' worlds: the published methods receive exactly the experience that the off-policy learner and the planner received. Their default warm-up is part of the budget: 100 random steps for SAC, less than one episode, and 1000 for TD-MPC2, five episodes of 200 periods if random proposals do not bring the cart to the work zone in time.
- **SAC and TD-MPC2, budget L: 1000 training episodes**, 25 passes over the same 40 worlds in the same order (episode i in world 100 s + i mod 40). An episode lasts at most 200 periods before arrival, and about 95 when the cart arrives in time, so L is about 10⁵ to 2 × 10⁵ environment steps. L is chosen so that the warm-up is a small part of training and the three seeds of each method fit within about a day of computing (README.md gives estimates); it is not chosen to make a method succeed, and no budget will be added after a run.
- The policy of budget M is the snapshot of the same training run after episode 40. Nothing in either algorithm depends on the total budget: their learning rates are constant, and TD-MPC2's buffer capacity, min(10⁶, 200 × 1000), binds in neither budget.
- **Diffusion policy: the chunk imitator's budget of demonstrations.** For seed s, five episodes in which the expert planner of Section 6.2.2 acts through the governor, in the worlds 100 s + 50, ..., + 54; one round of dataset aggregation, in which the policy trained on them acts in the worlds 100 s + 55, ..., + 59 and the expert labels the states it visits; the final policy trained anew on all records. The expert's episodes are those of the chunk imitator, since the expert and the worlds are the same; the aggregation episodes differ, because another policy acts. Each training uses the official schedule, 5000 epochs of batches of 256, so its number of gradient steps is 5000 times the number of batches of its data.
- **Seeds 1, 2 and 3** for every method. The seed sets the training worlds as above and seeds the library (SB3's `seed`; TD-MPC2's `set_seed` and its action space; torch, numpy and random for the diffusion policy).

## Evaluation

Every trained policy is frozen and run in the 20 evaluation worlds with seeds 1000 to 1019, which no training episode visits: SAC with its deterministic action (`predict(deterministic=True)`, the squashed mean of its actor); TD-MPC2 with `act(eval_mode=True)`, its planner's sampling seeded with the world's seed before each run; the diffusion policy with its EMA weights, its sampling seeded with the world's seed. The official diffusion workspace keeps the checkpoints with the best rollout scores; here no evaluation world is seen before a policy is frozen, and the last policy of training is the one evaluated. Proposals are on time: the environment does not model computation time. The measured inference time per proposal (median and largest) is recorded and reported beside the results, against the period of 0.1 s.

## Metrics

The same as for the stand-ins (`cart_learning.run_metrics` and `describe`, called by `protocol.py`): per run, the arrival time, the mean intervention (one minus the committed ambition, averaged exactly over the periods before arrival), fallback commits, alarms, steps outside a funnel, the largest position and the position at rest, periods committed in full, and, for one-reference proposals, periods in which the proposal passes unchanged and proposals at the largest acceleration. Per method and budget, pooled over the 60 evaluation runs of its three trained policies: the columns of Table 6.4 (runs arrived; mean, earliest and latest arrival; mean intervention; largest position over evaluation runs and training episodes), the mean intervention of each trained policy, and for the training episodes their number, arrivals, alarms, steps outside a funnel, the largest position, and the mean intervention of the first and the last ten. Operation timeliness: not applicable, because the task of Chapter 6 has no operation. The record of each run: the versions of Python and the packages, the commits of the checkouts, the GPU and CPU, the seeds, the environment steps and updates, the hyperparameters as the library holds them, the wall-clock times and `pip freeze`.

## Hypothesis H1: the guarantee during learning

The guarantees of Chapter 9 hold for every proposal method whenever the executed labels are valid (Proposition 6.1, part (b)), and every label of this world family is valid. Prediction: in every training episode and every evaluation run of the three methods, at every budget, the governor commits a plan in every period, no alarm is raised, no executed segment leaves its funnel, the cart never passes 24.5 m, and every run that arrives comes to rest. Decision: supported if there is no exception. An exception would show an error in the environment, the package or the test, not a property of a learner; it would be reported with the run, and the comparisons below would be withheld until its cause is found.

## Comparisons and their decision rule

The unit is the evaluation world. For each method and budget and each world, the mean over its three trained policies of the arrival time (infinite if one of them does not arrive there) and of the run's mean intervention, as printed with four decimals in the evaluation files. Two methods are compared world by world; ties are dropped, and the exact two-sided sign test gives p. "Earlier" (or "lower" for interventions) means that the method was better in more worlds and the decision's p is below 0.05; "later" (or "higher") the reverse; otherwise "no difference shown". Interventions are compared only between methods whose proposals have the same meaning (Section 6.2.3).

**Primary comparisons** (the family of eight tests, with Holm's correction at 0.05):

| | Comparison | Quantities |
|---|---|---|
| P1, P2 | SAC, budget M, against the off-policy learner | arrival; intervention |
| P3, P4 | SAC, budget L, against the off-policy learner | arrival; intervention |
| P5 | TD-MPC2, budget M, against the planner with a learned model | arrival |
| P6 | TD-MPC2, budget L, against the planner with a learned model | arrival |
| P7, P8 | the diffusion policy against the chunk imitator | arrival; intervention |

**Secondary comparisons** (uncorrected p, reported and never quoted as a shown difference): each method and budget against the rule, the braking curve and the expert planner in arrival; SAC and TD-MPC2 against the rule and the braking curve, and the diffusion policy against the expert planner, in intervention; TD-MPC2 against the off-policy learner in intervention, and against SAC in arrival and intervention; budget L against budget M for SAC and for TD-MPC2.

**What counts as better or worse.** A published method does better than its stand-in if its primary arrival test says "earlier", or if that test shows no difference and its intervention test (P2, P4, P8) says "lower"; it does worse if its arrival test says "later", or if that test shows no difference and its intervention test says "higher". Any other combination, for example earlier but with more interventions, is reported as it is, without a single ranking. Against the baselines, the same reading is applied to the secondary comparisons and stated as uncorrected. A method arrives in the protocol's sense only if all 60 of its evaluation runs arrive; any run that does not is reported. The comparisons concern these three trained policies of each method in these 20 worlds. They do not estimate the variation between training seeds, which three seeds cannot, and they rank nothing outside the cart, its world family and these budgets. They compare learners inside the same governed loop; none of them compares ALLOVI with anything.

## How the results will be reported, whatever they are

`report.py` writes `results/summary.txt` from the files of the runs: the table, the groups of each method and budget, the verdict of H1, the decisions of P1 to P8 with their counts and p-values, the secondary comparisons, and the record. Section 6.2.4 of the book will print the five rows (SAC M and L, TD-MPC2 M and L, the diffusion policy) with the columns of Table 6.4, the verdict of H1, the decisions of P1 to P8 as they come out, including "no difference shown" and decisions in favour of the stand-ins, and the inference times. A run that fails, a method that does not learn to arrive, or a crash is reported as such. No seed is replaced, no budget is extended and no hyperparameter is tuned after a run, unless the change is listed under *Deviations* with its reason, and then the results under both are reported. Runs made with `--smoke` test the installation and are not results.

## Deviations

1. **`report.py`, version 2 (29 October 2026), written after the SAC runs and while the TD-MPC2 runs went on.** Version 1 took Holm's correction over the primary tests whose files existed, and summarized whatever seeds of a method it found. This protocol takes the correction over the family of eight and the unit as the mean over the three trained policies in each world. Version 2 does both as written. While some of the eight tests have not been run, it gives the bounds of each adjusted p-value over every value that the missing tests could take, and states a decision only where the bounds allow it whatever those tests give (otherwise "pending"). It reports and compares a method only when all three seeds are in, and shows the seeds in so far as interim, apart from the results. It also prints p-values to three significant digits and an undefined mean arrival without a unit. Reason: version 1 would have reported decisions that the remaining tests could reverse, and a method from fewer seeds than the protocol's unit needs. With all eight tests and all seeds in, both versions decide alike; for SAC's P1 to P4 they decide alike now. The output of version 1 for the SAC runs is kept as `results/summary-report-v1.txt`. No training script, setting, seed, budget or decision rule changed.
2. **`train_sac.py`, option `--gamma` (29 October 2026), added after the SAC runs.** A reviewer pointed out that the comparison of SAC with the off-policy learner changes the discount factor (0.99, the library's default, against 0.5) as well as the algorithm. The option sets SAC's discount and writes to `results/sac-gamma<g>/`, which `report.py` does not read; without it the script runs exactly as before, and the registered runs, decisions and `report.py` are unchanged. The like-for-like check of the other direction, the stand-in at 0.99, is `../discount_check.py`. Both checks were chosen after SAC's results were known: they are exploratory and uncorrected, and the decisions of P1 to P4 remain those of the registered comparison.
3. **`reward_check.py` (29 October 2026), written after the TD-MPC2 runs.** TD-MPC2's policies mostly kept the cart at rest or did not bring it to the work zone within 20 s, and SAC's arrived later with more training. The script runs the rule, the braking curve, the off-policy learner and a policy that never accelerates through `cart_env` in the 20 evaluation worlds, adds up the rewards of each run, undiscounted and with the discounts of SAC (0.99) and TD-MPC2 (0.975), and bounds the undiscounted return of the published methods' runs that did not stop on the way, from their files (`results/reward-check.txt`). It tests nothing and decides nothing: it is exploratory, and no training script, setting, seed, budget, decision rule or decision of this protocol changed.
4. **A second protocol, `PROTOCOL-R2.md` (29 October 2026), written after the SAC and TD-MPC2 runs and before any run of its own.** It trains SAC and TD-MPC2 again with a reward that charges for time, −1 minus 0.5 times the intervention in every period before arrival, and gives TD-MPC2 the discount 0.99. For it, `cart_env.CartGovernedEnv` takes `reward="time"` (the default, `reward="registered"`, is this protocol's reward, unchanged), `train_sac.py` and `train_tdmpc2.py` take `--reward time` and write to `results/sac-time/` and `results/tdmpc2-time/`, and `report_r2.py` writes `results/summary-r2.txt`; without the option the scripts run exactly as before, `report.py` does not read those folders, and `test_cart_env.py` checks, in nine test runs, that the two rewards give the same run for the same proposals. The runs, decisions and summary of this protocol stand as reported, whatever the second protocol gives, and the two are reported together.
