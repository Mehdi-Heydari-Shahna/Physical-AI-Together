Record of the experiment of Section 15.2: the Go2 governed through a lift of its front-right foot

model and asset provenance
  MuJoCo Menagerie's unitree_go2/go2.xml, commit f3b6013629c7b94f8ee93fd977b161fa8350f9bc, BSD-3-Clause, derived by Menagerie from Unitree's URDF; its visual meshes, which carry no mass and no collision, deleted; a floor plane added (quad/model.py); the stance controller, the reduced model, the task, the modules and the runs ours (quad/)
simulator and solver
  MuJoCo 3.16.0; NumPy, SciPy and Python 2.5.3, 1.18.1, 3.13.17; go2.xml's options: Euler, elliptic cones, impratio 100, the feet's soft contacts of Menagerie
time steps
  the simulation 1 ms (go2.xml leaves MuJoCo's default of 2 ms); the governor's period 0.2 s; 50 periods, the robot settled 3 s before the first
actuators and sensors
  the twelve motors of go2.xml, torques within 23.7, 23.7 and 45.43 N m; the stance controller of quad/stance.py at every step; the measurement the simulated state, exact
frames and units
  SI units; world x forward, y left, z up; the state's place along the shift line from home towards the incentre of the triangle FL, RL, RR
uncertainty bounds
  the labels' bound on a push on the trunk (10 N, the global capability); the funnel's bounds, the allowance, its part per newton of push and the global capability: 0.008, 0.02; 0.005; 0.003; 10
constraints
  f4, the zero-moment point inside the rectangle of the four feet, at its floor; f3, inside the triangle of FL, RL, RR, with the fourth foot's credit C3 while FR carries its share; the levels: 0.02, 0.005, 0.02, 0.06, 0.08 m
terminal behaviour
  the rest on four feet, powered, a rest under the assumption that power and control last; after an alarm the rest feedback, which lowers FR at once; the shutdown construction run apart, not certified
configurations
  reference: the global capability, the lift announced in period 2
  pushed: pushes of 10 N across the triangle's long edge, on FR's side, in the shift, the hold and the touchdown
  unannounced: the lift not announced
  shove: a shove of 20 N in the pushes' direction in the hold, beyond the global capability
  obstacle: the floor under FR 30 mm higher than declared
  hole: the floor under FR 30 mm lower than declared
  band-higher: the floor under FR 10 mm higher, the band's edge
  band-lower: the floor under FR 10 mm lower, the band's edge
  ungoverned: the operator's proposal executed directly, with the pushed run's pushes
  ungoverned-calm: the same, without pushes
training and evaluation split
  none: no learned module
seeds
  none: no random numbers
hardware
  Linux x86_64, Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.80GHz
guarantee status
  tested in simulation: the certificate rests on the reduced model, the allowances and the funnel's bounds, which the runs, the sweep and every plan executed unchanged check in MuJoCo, in sample, and on the assumptions on the touchdown and on power; no physical robot
