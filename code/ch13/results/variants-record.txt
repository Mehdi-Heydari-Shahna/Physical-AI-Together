Record of the experiment of Section 13.3: the Husky A300 of Section 13.2 with one module substituted, or one assumption changed, at a time

model and asset provenance
  the model, the drive controller, the drivers and the declared contacts of Section 13.1 (record.txt); with the payload, 51.5 kg in a box of half sizes 0.2, 0.15 and 0.05 m, centred 0.3 m above the ground, 131.5 kg in all (ours, after the manual's payload)
simulator and solver
  MuJoCo 3.16.0, NumPy and Python 2.5.3, 3.13.17; the settings of Section 13.1
time steps
  simulator 0.0005 s; drive controller, estimator and feedback at 50 Hz; governor every 0.2 s; the crossing with pure pursuit at 0.5 m repeated at 1 and 0.25 ms
modules
  the reference of Section 13.2 (route-record.txt): control package husky.route-speed, proposal husky.cruise, predictor husky.map, the estimator with sideslip; substitutes (husky/variants.py): husky.route-speed-pursuit at look-aheads of 0.5, 0.75, 1 and 1.5 m, at 0.5 m with the stops and the crossings with the predictors of Section 13.2, at 1.5 m with the stops on the dry grounds; husky.hurry; and husky.careful over 300 periods
actuators and sensors
  as in Section 13.2; the drivers' torque limit at 30, 25, 20, 15, 13 and 10 N m in the stops on asphalt and at 20, 15, 12 and 10 N m in the crossing; the estimator's readings late by 0, 1, 2, 3 and 5 updates of 20 ms, and by 2 and 5 with the compensation; the torques recorded at every step of the simulator
frames and units
  as in Section 13.2
uncertainty bounds
  the bounds and labels of Section 13.2, unchanged in every run; the wet grass's true friction 0.35, 0.3, 0.25, 0.22, 0.2, 0.19, 0.18, 0.17, 0.16 and 0.15, in force from the start, under the map's label
constraints
  as in Section 13.2
terminal behaviour
  as in Section 13.2
training and evaluation split
  none: nothing learns in Section 13.3
seeds
  the gyro's noise, PCG64, seed 13; seeds 1 to 20 with pure pursuit at 0.5 m
hardware
  Linux x86_64, Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.80GHz
guarantee status
  that of Section 13.2 for the reference, for husky.hurry, whose plans are the reference's, and for pure pursuit at 0.5 m, which passed the same tests; for the other runs that kept every bound, the evidence of one crossing; for the others, the summary names the stated assumptions that the runs found broken, and there is no guarantee
