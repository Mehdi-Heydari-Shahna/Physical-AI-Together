  clearpath_common, https://github.com/clearpathrobotics/clearpath_common, commit 78e3677412f1fed881f6436cdaf404f04f28ee4f (19 October 2026), clearpath_platform_description and clearpath_control 2.9.18, BSD 3-Clause

simulator and solver
  MuJoCo 3.16.0, NumPy and Python 2.5.3, 3.13.17
  integrator = implicitfast; solver = Newton; friction cone = elliptic; iterations, tolerance = 100, 1e-08; impratio, noslip iterations = 100, 0
  wheel-ground pairs: solref, solimp, solreffriction = 0.008 1; 0.05 0.05 0.001 0.5 2; -0 -3 (friction damping 60 1/s)
  other contacts: MuJoCo's defaults (solref 0.02 1, solimp 0.9 0.95 0.001 0.5 2)
  settings that differ from MuJoCo's defaults: time step, integrator, friction cone, impratio (defaults: 0.002 s, euler, pyramidal, 1)
time steps
  simulator 0.0005 s; drive controller at 50 Hz, one every 40 steps; drivers' speed loops at every step; the stops, spins and turns repeated at 1 and 0.25 ms
actuators and sensors
  drive controller: diff_drive_controller (ros2_controllers, jazzy, d71703b) as ported in husky/drive.py, configuration diff_4wd.yaml: 50, 0.562 x 1.75, 0.1625
  drivers (ours): speed loop, proportional gain, integral time and torque limit 40, 0.1, 30
  sensors: four wheel encoders (positions, speeds), a 6-axis IMU at the chassis' centre (ours: the description places none); the chassis pose from the simulator
frames and units
  SI units; world x east, y north, z up, ground at z = 0; base_link x forward, y left; route arc length s, lateral offset d positive to the left
uncertainty bounds
  frictions of the grounds (the simulator's, not measured): asphalt 0.8, gravel 0.6, wet grass 0.35; no labels yet (Section 13.2)
constraints
  lane 1.4 m wide centred on the route, to hold the robot's footprint (0.990 m x 0.698 m); pallets 0.1 m outside its edges; the dock's face at s = 25 m; speed at most 1 m/s on the route; controller limits 2, 2, 4
terminal behaviour
  a stop commanded through the controller (deceleration limit 4 m/s^2)
training and evaluation split
  none: nothing learns in Section 13.1
seeds
  none: no random numbers
