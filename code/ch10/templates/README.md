# Templates of modules (Section 10.3)

One file for each of the eight roles of Table 10.1, and one for a robot model (`robot_model.py`, Section 10.4), which is not a role but the record through which a model provider delivers the description of the robot. `HELPERS.md` documents, with examples, what a module of the cart may reuse beyond the interfaces of the templates (the cart's units, constants, state and proposals, its task, its segments and plans, the functions of its generator and its control package) and how to run and check a module in the cart's configurations; read it with the template of your role. To contribute a module:

1. Copy the template of its role, rename the class, set `NAME` (unique within the role, for example `yourname.method`), and fill in every method. The docstrings state what each method receives and returns; `../allovi/contract.py` is the reference, and `HELPERS.md` gives the shapes and units of the cart's data.
2. Add `@register` (from `allovi`) above the class. Registration checks the role, the name, the version of the contract, and every method with its number of positional parameters.
3. Write down the obligations of the role that the docstring of the template lists, and how you establish each: by a proof, by evidence, or by the specification of the hardware. No check of the software discharges them.
4. Run the checks of Section 10.3 on the cart with your module in its role: `python3 ../cart_checks.py --module path/to/your_module.py`. The script creates each module that the file registers without arguments, and imports the file with only `code/ch10` and `code/ch03` on the path. For a module whose constructor needs an argument, such as a demand predictor that reads the cart's task, for a file that imports another package, such as the benchmark's `bench` of Chapter 16, for a task, which the cart's demand predictor and evaluator cannot take, and for a module of another robot, call `allovi.checks.check` with a function that builds your configuration. `HELPERS.md` gives the call; it is the one that `../../ch17/contribute.py` makes for its two contributions.

The steps that support a contribution's claim, registered hypotheses, a matched comparison, a report of every outcome and a method card, are in Chapter 17 and in `../../ch17` (`README.md`, `cards/TEMPLATE.md`, `templates/`); the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` gives the whole path, up to a pull request.

The templates are not registered, so that importing them registers nothing; `../cart_checks.py --check` confirms that each of them still has every method of its role.
